Questions

Can I enter more than one category?

One entry per person, but a solo cosplayer can also be part of one group.

Do finalists need an event ticket?

No. All 20 finalists get a free 2-Day Event Pass.

Can I use bought costumes?

Yes, but handmade work scores higher on craftsmanship (40% of the score).
