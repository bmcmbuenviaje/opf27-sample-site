When do tickets go on sale?

February 2027 on tickets.otakupopfes.com. Get notified.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable unless the event is cancelled.

Do kids need a ticket?

Kids 0–6 enter free with a paying adult (Event Pass areas only).

What time do doors open?

10:00 both days. Wristband claiming starts at 09:30 at Event Arc 1.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes, on the same day with your wristband.

Where can I change into my cosplay?

Changing rooms are next to the Grand Ballroom, open 09:30–20:00.

Are prop weapons allowed?

Yes if they're under 2 m and not made of metal or sharp materials. Staff check props at entry.

Is there a bag check?

All bags are checked at entry. Bag storage is available at ₱100 per item.

Can I bring food and drinks?

Sealed water is allowed. Food is available at Food Street.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes. All halls have ramps and elevators. PWD lanes are at every entrance.

Is there a quiet room?

Yes, beside the Info Desk in the Crystal Pavilion.

I lost something. What do I do?

Go to the Info Desk in the Crystal Pavilion, or message us after the event.
