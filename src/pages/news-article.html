🌀 Otaku Pop Fes 2027 is isekai-bound! Save the date: June 5–6, 2027

Pack your bags, travellers. Otaku Pop Fes returns on June 5–6, 2027 at Okada Manila — and this time, one venue opens into three different worlds.

Three worlds, one weekend

Discover IP booths and the artist market in the Crystal Pavilion. Connect with fellow fans at panels and signings in the Grand Ballroom. Celebrate into the night at COVE Music Live.

  • Lineup reveals start in January. Get updates so you don't miss them.

See you in the new worlds!
