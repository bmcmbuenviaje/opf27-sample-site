{{title rules-title Categories}}
Solo — Anime & Manga

One cosplayer, character from an anime or manga. Max 90 seconds on stage. Props up to 2 m.

Solo — Games

One cosplayer, character from a video or mobile game. Max 90 seconds on stage.

Group (2–5 members)

Characters from the same series. Max 3 minutes including setup. Pre-recorded audio allowed (MP3, submitted in advance).

Kids (12 and under)

Parent or guardian must register and be present backstage.
