{{title gt-title Getting There}}
By shuttle bus

Free OPF shuttles run every 20 minutes from 08:30 to 22:30.

Route A: SM Mall of Asia (Bus Bay 3) ↔ Okada Manila
Route B: PITX Terminal ↔ Okada Manila
Route C: Baclaran LRT-1 ↔ Okada Manila

Routes are placeholders

By Grab or taxi

Set your drop-off to Okada Manila Fountain Foyer.

By car

Take NAIA Expressway, exit at Entertainment City / Aseana. Follow signs to Okada Manila.

By public transport

LRT-1 to Baclaran or EDSA, then the OPF shuttle (Route C) or a jeepney to PITX.
