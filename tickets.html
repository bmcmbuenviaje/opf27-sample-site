FAQFAQFAQ

What does the "~" after a price mean?

Prices shown are before 12% VAT and the ticketing platform's service fee. You'll see the final total at checkout before you pay.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable unless the event is cancelled. See the Ticket Terms.

Can I transfer my ticket to a friend?

Yes, once, up to 7 days before the event, from your ticketing account.

Is re-entry allowed?

Yes, on the same day, with your wristband.

Do I need a separate ticket for Music Live?

Yes. Music Live needs an Event + Music Live pass or a VIP pass.
