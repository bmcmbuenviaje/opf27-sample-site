ConceptConceptConcept

Get Isekaied. Experience New Worlds.

From the heart of Japan to Okada Manila, Otaku Pop Fes (OPF) returns on June 5–6, 2027 — a two-day celebration where one venue opens into different worlds of anime, games, cosplay and music.

Collect stamps in your OPF Companion as you discover IP experience booths and the artist market, connect with fellow fans at panels and fan sessions, then celebrate into the night at COVE Music Live. Where to next?

One Venue, Different Worlds

Discover

Crystal Pavilion

IPs · Market · Food · Discovery

Twenty IP experience booths, the artist market, a Dotonbori-inspired Food Street and the Crystal Stage — home of the Cosplay Cup and Ani-Karaoke.

Connect

Grand Ballroom

Panels · Community · Conversation

Anime and creator panels, screenings, fan sessions and signings — plus the Artist Alley, Doujin District and Official Merchandise zone.

Celebrate

Cove Manila

Music Live · Ticket access applies

COVE Music Live lights up both nights with anisong and J-pop acts. Admission starts at 17:30; a Music Live pass is required.

Meet the O-Trio

Otori

The World-Hopping Guide

Your adventurous fandom bestie. She travels between worlds collecting experiences in her festival passport — energetic, curious, and medyo madaling ma-distract ng cute merch.

Popi

The Festival Spirit

A lantern spirit who takes human form whenever fans gather. Siya yung welcoming, makulit na host who makes everyone feel part of the celebration.

Fumi

The Story Maker

A sketchbook spirit who brings imagined worlds to life. Creative, witty and a little cheeky, she encourages fans to create their own stories.
