Can someone else collect my pre-order?

Yes — they just need your pickup pass (screenshot is fine) and your name.

What if I can't come to the event?

If you paid, we'll email you on June 7 to arrange delivery to your address; a shipping fee applies. Unpaid reservations are released after the Day 1 cut-off.

How do I pay?

Online through Slash, or at the Merch Hub counters on the Slash card terminal.

Can I change sizes?

At pickup, if the size you want is still in stock.
