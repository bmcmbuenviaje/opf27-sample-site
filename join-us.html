How to JoinHow to JoinHow to Join

  1. Choose your booth

    Compare booth types, inclusions and rates above. Not sure which fits? Email the exhibitor desk.

  2. Submit the form

    Tell us about your brand, art or menu and attach photos of what you'll sell.

  3. Get screened

    We review every application for curation and IP compliance, then email your result and invoice within 3–5 business days.

  4. Secure your slot

    Pay the 50% reservation fee within 5 business days. The balance is due April 30, 2027.

  5. Set up & shine

    Receive your booth assignment, exhibitor passes and ingress schedule. Load-in is June 4, 2027.
